## 1. Purpose

Record **personal data processing** activities for iConnect Plus under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)** and related internal policies. This deployment processes data **on-premises in India** at Cache Digitech premises; remote access is via **corporate VPN**.

## 8. Cross-border transfer

Default configuration: **no intentional cross-border transfer** of primary OLTP/MinIO data. Optional integrations (email, Entra, FCM) may transfer metadata — disable or contract if not permitted.
