Who this manual is for and access

This manual is for Projects module admins and assigned members (delivery, survey, SCM, installation, and PMO contributors who receive My Jobs tasks).

Sign in with Microsoft Entra SSO. Passwords are not stored in iConnect Plus for Entra users. If Projects is missing from your module hub, ask an admin to assign you on Projects - Users.
  1. Open iConnect Plus and complete Entra sign-in.
  2. Open the Projects module from the ERP module hub.
  3. Use the left sidebar to move between panes. Collapse the sidebar when you need more table space.
  4. Work owned delivery steps from My Jobs first each day.
Business rule: site projects for customer POs start in PO Queue (admin). Each pipeline stage advances only when the assigned owner submits the stage form in My Jobs.

Part A - Process walkthrough

Follow these steps in order. Use the demo names below so your captures match this manual.

  • Customer: Airtel
  • Project: H2 26-27 - Spectrum Santhome
  • CRM link: PRJ-2026-000003 - customer PO 97210 (H-Cloud / installation PO Queue seed)
  • Site: Spectrum Santhome (SITE-2026-000003)
  • Seed: H-Cloud demo PO row is valid when staging sync is pending

Upstream context: use a PO Queue seed or shared installation PO so 97210 exists before you open PO Queue.

Later stage forms (SCM through Acceptance) show the same stage layout on an in-progress site when the Spectrum Santhome survey is still open - field labels and step numbers match the live pipeline.

Step 01 - PO Queue

Open PO Queue (admin). Locate the shared installation PO from CRM: 97210 for H2 26-27 - Spectrum Santhome (Airtel). H-Cloud seed data is acceptable for the walkthrough when the live PO row is not yet synced.

Step 02 - Create project form

Choose Create Project on the PO row. Confirm project title H2 26-27 - Spectrum Santhome, site code Spectrum Santhome, and CRM link PRJ-2026-000003 / PO 97210. Save to open project intake.

Step 03 - Project detail - intake

On the project header, verify customer, PO reference, and delivery pipeline stage Intake. Use Project Tracking to see the next assignable steps.

Step 04 - Assign survey owner

From Project Tracking, assign the Survey step owner (demo: field survey lead). The assignee receives a My Jobs task.

Step 05 - My Jobs - survey task

The survey owner opens My Jobs, finds the Survey step for the campus site, and opens the step form.

Step 06 - Survey form

Complete site survey fields: access, rack locations, cable paths, and photos/notes. Submit to advance the site to SCM planning.

Step 07 - Assign SCM owner

Admin assigns the SCM / Logistics step owner after survey completes. Material planning and vendor alignment happen in this stage.

Step 08 - SCM form

Enter BOM lines, expected dispatch dates, and logistics remarks. Submit when material is cleared for onsite delivery.

Step 09 - Onsite delivery form

Record onsite receipt: delivery challan reference, serials received, and site contact sign-off. Submit to unlock material handover.

Step 10 - Material handover form

Document handover from logistics to installation team: quantities, condition, and storage location on campus.

Step 11 - Installation form

Capture installation and configuration progress: AP mounts, switch stacks, controller join, and test SSID results.

Step 12 - Acceptance form

Customer acceptance: UAT checklist, punch-list closure, and acceptance sign-off. Submit to move the site to Completed.

Step 13 - Completed Jobs

Finished delivery steps appear under Completed Jobs for read-only review by the owner and admins.

Step 14 - Projects list

Open Projects register. Filter for H2 26-27 - Spectrum Santhome and confirm stage, site count, and owner columns match tracker expectations.

Step 15 - Tracker

Admins use Tracker for versioned Excel-like rollout tables (sites, dates, and stage columns) across the program.

Step 16 - All Sites

All Sites shows every site installation row by pipeline stage. Use it to find stalled sites and reassign owners.

Step 17 - Project tasks

Classic PMO Project Tasks (WBS) supports phases beyond the installation pipeline - use for internal coordination tasks.

Step 18 - Timesheets

Members log effort on assigned tasks via Timesheets for cost and utilization reporting.

Step 19 - Budgets

Project Budgets compare planned vs actual spend against the commercial baseline for 97210.

Step 20 - Risks

Register delivery risks (access delays, OEM lead time, weather) on Risks with owners and mitigation plans.

Step 21 - Change requests

Scope or commercial changes use Change Requests with approval before tracker and budget baselines move.

Roles and responsibilities

Module admin

  • Create projects from PO Queue
  • Assign stage owners on Project Tracking
  • Monitor Dashboard, Tracker, and All Sites
  • Manage Entra users on Users
  • Approve change requests and rebaseline budgets

Delivery member

  • Complete owned steps from My Jobs
  • Submit survey, SCM, and installation forms
  • Review history in Completed Jobs
  • Log timesheets on assigned tasks
  • Escalate blockers to the project admin

Project manager

  • Own WBS tasks and milestones
  • Maintain risks and issues
  • Track budget vs actuals
  • Coordinate cross-stage dependencies
  • Report portfolio status to leadership

Survey / field

  • Own Survey stage forms
  • Capture site readiness and constraints
  • Attach photos and measurement notes
  • Hand off to SCM with clear BOM hints

SCM / logistics

  • Own SCM and onsite delivery stages
  • Align material dispatch to survey BOM
  • Record challan and serial receipt
  • Support material handover to installation

Installation / acceptance

  • Own installation configuration forms
  • Run UAT and customer acceptance
  • Close punch-list items before sign-off
  • Confirm Completed stage on the site
