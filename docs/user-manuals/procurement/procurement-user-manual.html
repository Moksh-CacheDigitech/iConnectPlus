Who this manual is for and access

This manual is for Procurement module admins and assigned SCM members who work OVFs, vendor POs, GRNs, and delivery.

Sign in with Microsoft Entra SSO. Passwords are not stored in iConnect Plus for Entra users. If Procurement is missing from your module hub, ask an admin to assign you on Procurement - Users.
  1. Open iConnect Plus and complete Entra sign-in.
  2. Open the Procurement module from the ERP module hub.
  3. Use the left sidebar (Workspace + Insight). Collapse the sidebar when you need more table space.
  4. Start each day from SCM Queue and Approval.
Business rule: customer OVFs enter Procurement from CRM. Vendor POs are raised from the OVF item plan (or inventory Create PO). GRN and Billing/DC close the physical delivery loop.

Part A - Process walkthrough

Follow these steps in order. Use the demo names below so your captures match this manual.

  • Customer: Walkthrough Demo Systems Pvt Ltd
  • Project: Walkthrough Demo - Campus WiFi FY26
  • OVF: DR-2026-8/OVF1
  • Customer PO: WDS-PO-FY26-001
  • Vendor / OEM: Redington / Cisco
  • Example issued PO (GRN path): PO/CDT/068

Upstream: complete the CRM walkthrough so the OVF is approved and shared to SCM before you open Procurement.

Step 01 - SCM Queue

Open SCM Queue. Find the OVF shared from CRM: DR-2026-8/OVF1 for Walkthrough Demo Systems Pvt Ltd (vendor Redington). Filter Open when the list is long.

Step 02 - View OVF

Open View OVF. Confirm customer PO WDS-PO-FY26-001, project Walkthrough Demo - Campus WiFi FY26, OEM Cisco, margin, and attached sales documents. Use Hold only when procurement must pause.

Step 03 - Item plan

Choose Create PO to open the item plan. Select distributor per line (demo: Redington) and review quantities before raising the vendor PO.

Step 04 - Fulfill from stock (optional)

When stock covers demand, open Fulfill from inventory. Reserve units against the OVF first - this does not create a delivery challan by itself.

Step 05 - Create purchase order

Complete the Create PO form: vendor, entity, billing and shipping addresses, company PO number, and purchase date. Save to open the draft or pending-approval PO.

Step 06 - Purchase Orders register

Open Purchase Orders to track draft, open, partial, and closed vendor POs. Later steps in this manual use an issued example PO (PO/CDT/068) for GRN and delivery screens.

Step 07 - PO detail and delivery tracking

Open a PO row (demo example PO/CDT/068). Capture expected delivery date, run correspondence, download the PO PDF, and use Record GRN when goods arrive.

Step 08 - Approval

Admins use Approval for Create PO (IN STOCK) and finalize-PO requests. Accepting a request issues the draft PO - sharing to Projects happens later from Installation.

Step 09 - GRNs

Record receipts on GRNs. Partial vs delivered status drives Delivery Status and Installation handoff. Export Excel when auditors need a snapshot.

Step 10 - Billing / delivery challan

Use Billing/DC to create delivery challans (deliver without a bill) or billing rows. Update bill taken on saved DCs at any time.

Step 11 - Delivery status

After dispatch is saved, open Delivery Status and use Update status to mark Delivered or Failed. Bill update is available only for fully billed DCs.

Step 12 - Inventory

Check Inventory for units, OEM coverage, and stock value before allocating from stock on an OVF. Add stock or import Excel when warehouse balances change.

Step 13 - Timeline list

Open Insight - Timeline for every OVF from SCM handoff through GRN, challan, dispatch, and delivery completion.

Step 14 - OVF timeline detail

Open the timeline for DR-2026-8/OVF1 to audit actors and timestamps (shared to SCM, vendor PO, GRN, challan, dispatch, billing).

Roles and responsibilities

Module admin

  • Monitor Dashboard, Analytics, and Reports
  • Approve Create PO / finalize requests
  • Manage Vendors, Inventory, and Users
  • Hold / unhold OVFs when needed
  • Own Delivery Projects and Tracker Upload

SCM buyer

  • Work SCM Queue and item plans
  • Create vendor POs and chase ETD
  • Record GRNs and delivery challans
  • Update Delivery Status after dispatch
  • Use Mails / Timeline for audit

Warehouse / logistics

  • Maintain Inventory balances
  • Allocate from stock on OVFs
  • Confirm serials on GRN
  • Prepare Billing/DC paperwork
  • Flag stock aging and warranty risks
