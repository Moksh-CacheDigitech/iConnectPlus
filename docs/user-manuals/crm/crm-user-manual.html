Who this manual is for and access

This manual is for CRM module admins and assigned members (sales, pre-sales, and approvers who receive My Jobs tasks).

Sign in with Microsoft Entra SSO. Passwords are not stored in iConnect Plus for Entra users. If CRM is missing from your module hub, ask an admin to assign you on CRM - Users.
  1. Open iConnect Plus and complete Entra sign-in.
  2. Open the CRM module from the ERP module hub.
  3. Use the left sidebar to move between panes. Collapse the sidebar when you need more table space.
  4. Work owned tasks from My Jobs first each day.
Business rule: create a Company account before you create a Lead. Attaching BOQ and SOW in My Jobs drives conversion to an Opportunity.

Part A - Process walkthrough

Follow these steps in order. Use the demo company and project names below so your captures match this manual.

  • Company: Walkthrough Demo Systems Pvt Ltd (COMP-111)
  • Project / deal title: Walkthrough Demo - Campus WiFi FY26
  • Contact: Ms. Ananya Verma (IT Head)
  • Deal reference: DR-2026-8 · Quote DR-2026-8/Q1 · OVF DR-2026-8/OVF1 · PO WDS-PO-FY26-001
  • Won amount: ₹4,50,155 (23.33% quote margin)

Sample PDFs for attachments live in assets/demo-files/ (demo-boq.pdf, demo-sow.pdf, demo-oem-quote.pdf, demo-customer-po.pdf).

Step 01 - Company list

Open Company. Create the end-customer account before any lead. Demo code COMP-111 is Walkthrough Demo Systems Pvt Ltd.

Step 02 - Create company form

Fill legal name, GST, address (Pune), and industry. Save the company so leads can link to it.

Step 03 - Company detail

Confirm the saved company record, contacts, and entity mapping before creating the lead.

Step 04 - Create lead form

Create a lead on the company. Set project title Walkthrough Demo - Campus WiFi FY26, contact Ms. Ananya Verma (IT Head), expected order value ₹4,50,000, OEM Cisco, and CapEx.

Step 05 - Lead detail

Open lead DR-2026-8. Confirm BOQ and SOW are required, then route work through My Jobs.

Step 06 - My Jobs - BOQ and SOW

Open My Jobs. Complete the BOQ and SOW tasks for this lead by attaching the demo PDFs.

Step 07 - Submit BOQ

Attach demo-boq.pdf and submit the BOQ package with a short remark.

Step 08 - Submit SOW

Attach demo-sow.pdf and submit the SOW. Both attachments unlock conversion to an opportunity.

Step 09 - Opportunities list

Open Opportunities. The converted deal appears as Walkthrough Demo - Campus WiFi FY26 (DR-2026-8).

Step 10 - Opportunity detail

Open the opportunity. Blueprint actions drive the rest of the path: vendor quote, customer quote, PO, OVF, Won.

Step 11 - Attach vendor quote

Choose Vendor Quotation Received, then attach demo-oem-quote.pdf (OEM Cisco / distributor pricing).

Step 12 - Create quote form

Choose Create Quote. Enter HSN, unit cost, quantity, and margin % so selling price is cost plus markup (demo: 10 x ₹36,500 at 23.33% margin).

Step 13 - Quote detail

Review quote DR-2026-8/Q1 totals (₹4,50,155) and attachments before internal approval.

Step 14 - Send quote for approval

Click Send for Approval. Select an approver (demo: Moksh Sharma) and add a remark.

Step 15 - Approve quote in My Jobs

Approver opens My Jobs, reviews margin, and approves the quote task.

Step 16 - Quotation sent to customer

After approval, run Quotation Sent to Customer. Status moves to Quote Sent.

Step 17 - Quote accepted

When the customer accepts, choose Quote Accepted and confirm with a remark. Quote stage becomes accepted.

Step 18 - Attach customer PO

Open opportunity Attachments and upload demo-customer-po.pdf (or use blueprint Attach Customer PO).

Step 19 - Customer PO attached

Confirm demo-customer-po.pdf appears with type Customer Po alongside BOQ, SOW, and OEM quote.

Step 20 - Customer PO on Purchase Order pane

Open the opportunity Purchase Order pane. The customer PO document is linked and enters the Finance → Legal → Management approval chain after Send PO for Approval.

Step 21 - Finance PO validation

In My Jobs, approve Validate Customer PO (Finance - tax & commercials). The opportunity then shows Finance as Approved.

Step 22 - Legal PO validation

Approve Validate Customer PO Terms & Conditions (Legal).

Step 23 - Management PO approval

Approve Approve Customer PO (Management). All three checks unlock Create OVF.

Step 24 - PO approved - Create OVF unlocked

Back on the opportunity, Customer PO Validation shows all three checks Approved. Quote stage is Accepted and OVF can be created (demo already shows linked OVF after create).

Step 25 - OVF module fields

On create/edit OVF, enter PO number WDS-PO-FY26-001, PO date, payment terms, and distributor (Redington). Save to open the OVF detail.

Step 26 - OVF created

OVF DR-2026-8/OVF1 opens with customer and vendor PO summaries filled from the quote.

Step 27 - Send OVF for approval

Click Send for Approval, select approver, and submit with a remark. After approval the OVF can proceed to Deal Won.

Step 28 - OVF approved

Approve Approve OVF DR-2026-8/OVF1 in My Jobs. The OVF remains linked on the opportunity.

Step 29 - Ready to mark Deal Won

Return to the opportunity. After OVF approval, blueprint action Mark Deal Won is available (demo capture shows the deal after Won with OVF linked).

Step 30 - Deal Won amount confirmed

Confirm Deal Won amount (demo ₹4,50,155) and submit. OVF shows the Won amount banner.

Step 31 - Deal Won

Opportunity status is Deal Won. Quote DR-2026-8/Q1 and OVF DR-2026-8/OVF1 remain linked for audit.

Roles and responsibilities

Sales

  • Own leads and opportunities
  • Attach BOQ/SOW and customer PO
  • Send quotes to customers
  • Update deal stage through Won

Pre-sales

  • Prepare BOQ and SOW in My Jobs
  • Map vendor quotes to BOQ lines
  • Support quote revisions
  • Validate technical scope on OVF

Management

  • Approve quotes in My Jobs
  • Review pipeline on Dashboard
  • Monitor sales performance reports
  • Escalate stalled approvals

Finance

  • Margin and payment-term approval
  • PO value verification
  • OVF billing alignment
  • Stock aging reviews when needed

Legal

  • Contract and SOW compliance
  • PO terms review in approval chain
  • KYC document checks
  • Entity master alignment

Admin

  • Assign Entra users on Users
  • Maintain products, OEM, distributor
  • Configure entities and templates
  • Support reporting and exports
