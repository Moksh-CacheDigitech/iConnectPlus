Who this manual is for and access

This manual is for HRMS admins and assigned HR members who hire, manage attendance and leave, run payroll, and process exits.

Sign in with Microsoft Entra SSO. Passwords are not stored in iConnect Plus for Entra users. If HRMS is missing from your module hub, ask a Superadmin to grant access on Superadmin Panel.
  1. Open iConnect Plus and complete Entra sign-in.
  2. Open the HRMS module from the ERP module hub.
  3. Use the left sidebar. Expand Org Setup and IT & Admin for nested panes.
  4. Start configuration from Org Setup before bulk hiring.
Business rule: employees can enter via Recruitment - Onboarding or direct Add Employee. Attendance and payroll depend on an Active employment record.

HR lifecycle (demo walkthrough)

Part A - Process walkthrough

Follow these steps in order. Demo profile used in screenshots:

  • Employee: Moksh Sharma
  • Code: CT5345
  • Designation: Junior AI Developer
  • Department: Technical
  • Entity: Cache Digitech

Step 01 - Recruitment

Open Recruitment to create job openings, add candidates, run the pipeline, schedule interviews, and generate offers. After offer acceptance, continue in Onboarding.

Step 02 - Onboarding

Start an onboarding case from Onboarding. Track invitations, forms, document verification, and ready-to-join status before the employee appears as Active.

Step 03 - Employees directory

Open Employees for the workforce register. Filter by entity, department, and designation. Use Add Employee for direct hire when recruitment is not used.

Step 04 - Add Employee wizard

Complete the nine-step wizard: Personal, Employment, Government IDs, Bank, Education, Previous Job, Salary, Documents, and Review. You can also load details from an onboarding case.

Step 05 - Employee profile

Open a profile (demo: Moksh Sharma / CT5345). Tabs cover Overview, Employment, Gov IDs, Bank, Docs, Assets, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, and Offboarding.

Step 06 - Attendance

Use Attendance to mark daily attendance, review missing punches and late arrivals, or explore by employee and calendar. Biometric devices sync punches into this register.

Step 07 - Performance

In Performance, create review cycles, assign goals and KPIs, run appraisals, and track PIP or probation when needed.

Step 08 - Training

Schedule programs under Training, assign attendees, and track planned vs completed sessions.

Step 09 - Payroll

Open Payroll for salary structures, monthly runs, approvals, month lock, revisions, bonuses, loans, and payslips.

Step 10 - Employee Requests

Managers and HR review team requests on Employee Requests: attendance corrections, on tour / on duty, leaves, and other ESS items.

Step 11 - Org Setup

Configure branches, departments, designations, grades, and legal entities under Org Setup - Organisation before hiring at scale.

Step 12 - Leave Setup

Define leave types, holiday calendars, and attendance policy under Org Setup - Leave Setup.

Step 13 - Offboarding

Raise exits on Offboarding: on notice, clearance, exit interview, documents, and FNF. Direct exits can skip notice when policy allows.

Roles and responsibilities

HR admin / Superadmin

  • Own Org Setup and Leave Setup
  • Grant HRMS access on Superadmin Panel
  • Approve payroll month lock and sensitive exits
  • Configure biometric devices and policies

HR executive

  • Run Recruitment and Onboarding
  • Maintain Employees and EDoc
  • Process Employee Requests
  • Coordinate Offboarding clearance

Manager / payroll ops

  • Approve team leave and attendance requests
  • Assign goals and complete reviews
  • Run payroll cycles and payslips
  • Book meeting rooms and travel as needed
