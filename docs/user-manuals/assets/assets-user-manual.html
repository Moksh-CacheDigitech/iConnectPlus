Who this manual is for and access

This manual is for Asset Management admins and assigned domain members who receive, register, issue, transfer, maintain, and dispose of IT and Non-IT assets.

Sign in with Microsoft Entra SSO. Passwords are not stored in iConnect Plus for Entra users. If Asset Management is missing from your module hub, ask a Superadmin to grant domain membership on Users.
  1. Open iConnect Plus and complete Entra sign-in.
  2. Open Asset Management from the ERP module hub.
  3. Choose IT Assets or Non-IT Assets under Domains.
  4. Configure Asset Types, Locations, and Departments before large-scale intake.
Business rule: assets can enter via Incoming - QC - Registration or direct Add Asset. Assignment and DC Challan track custody after the asset is on the register.

Part A - Process walkthrough

Follow these steps in order. Demo asset used in screenshots:

  • Asset: CT-HP-Aman
  • Tag: AST-2026-000003
  • Make / Model: HP · HP ELITEBOOK 840 G7
  • Assignee: Aman Raj (CT5319)
  • Location: New Delhi - CRC2
  • Assignment: AASN-2026-000002

Step 01 - All Assets inventory

Open All Assets for the IT register. Search by tag, serial, make, model, or assignee. Filter by operational status (Ready, Assigned, Maintenance, Disposed).

Step 02 - Incoming Assets

Record shipments under Incoming Assets when stock arrives from vendors or branches. Capture packing references before QC.

Step 03 - Incoming QC

Inspect received units on Incoming QC. Pass or fail serials, note defects, and release approved units toward registration.

Step 04 - Pending Registration

Complete tag, type, make, and model on Pending Registration so the asset enters the live register.

Step 05 - Add Asset

Use + Add Asset for direct registration when intake is not required. Enter identity, configuration, location, and accessories.

Step 06 - Asset Assignment

Issue assets to employees on Asset Assignment. Track assignee, employee ID, location, assignment date, and DC Challan status.

Step 07 - DC Challan

Create and track delivery challans on DC Challan (Pending - Sent to SCM - Document received - Signed - Received). Independent of assignment delivery references.

Step 08 - Transfers

Move assets between locations on Transfers. Documents progress Draft - Submitted - Approved - Completed (or Cancelled).

Step 09 - Maintenance

Open work orders on Maintenance. Record reason, expected duration, and return date. Use Show history for closed jobs.

Step 10 - Disposal

Retire assets on Disposal. View reason, approval, and disposal date from the disposed register.

Step 11 - Components

Install chargers, batteries, and other accessories on Components. Link lightweight types or attach a registered child asset to a parent.

Step 12 - Documents

Attach warranties, invoices, and photos from Documents. Open View Documents on an asset row (demo: three files on CT-HP-Aman).

Step 13 - Reports

Review analytics on Reports: document coverage, components, assignment usage, and lifecycle open counts - separate from the operational dashboard.

Step 14 - Non-IT Assets

Switch domain to Non-IT Assets for furniture, facilities, and other non-IT inventory. Use Inventory, Types, Locations, and Users under that domain.

Step 15 - Asset detail drawer

From All Assets, open View on an item (demo: CT-HP-Aman / AST-2026-000003). The drawer shows Overview, Assignment, IT Information, Location, Accessories, Delivery Challan, Remarks, and Assignment history.

Roles and responsibilities

Asset admin / Superadmin

  • Own types, locations, and departments
  • Grant IT / Non-IT domain memberships
  • Approve disposals and sensitive transfers
  • Configure reports and retention policies

IT asset operator

  • Run Incoming, QC, and Registration
  • Issue assignments and DC challans
  • Log maintenance and components
  • Attach warranties and invoices

Facilities / Non-IT

  • Maintain Non-IT inventory
  • Update types and locations
  • Coordinate furniture and facility moves
  • Support disposal of non-IT stock
