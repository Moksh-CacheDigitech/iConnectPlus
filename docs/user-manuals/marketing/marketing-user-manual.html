Who this manual is for and access

This manual is for Marketing & Social module admins, content creators, and approval heads who plan campaigns, produce deliverables, and publish across channels.

Sign in with Microsoft Entra SSO. Passwords are not stored in iConnect Plus for Entra users. If Marketing & Social is missing from your module hub, ask a module admin to assign you on Users.
  1. Open iConnect Plus and complete Entra sign-in.
  2. Open Marketing & Social from the ERP module hub.
  3. Use the left sidebar groups: Workspace, Content, Insights, Channels, and Admin.
  4. Set Brand kit before large Content Studio runs so drafts stay on-brand.
Business rule: only Marketing head and module admins can create campaigns. Creators submit deliverables; approval heads approve on Requests before calendar publish.

Part A - Process walkthrough

Follow these steps in order. Demo records used in screenshots:

Step 01 - Overview

Open Overview for campaign, request, calendar, and intelligence KPIs plus workspace quick links into Operations, Tasks, Content Studio, Campaigns, and more.

Step 02 - Operations

Use Operations for campaign health, delayed work, and utilization. Review per-campaign task completion before escalating bottlenecks.

Step 03 - My Work

Creators open My Work to attach brochure or video links and submit deliverables for approval.

Step 04 - Campaigns list

Module admins and Marketing heads create and track campaigns on Campaigns. Demo campaign: etciso (MKT-00002).

Step 05 - Reply inbox

After live publishes, use Inbox to pull comments and mentions. Stub publishes do not create reply threads.

Step 06 - Tasks board

The Tasks pane lists deliverables across campaigns. Toggle My tasks vs All tasks, open a row to submit work, then wait for approval.

Step 07 - Workload

Managers use Workload for capacity, utilization, and overload signals before reassigning work.

Step 08 - Content Studio

Generate platform-ready drafts in Content Studio with topic, tone, creative brief, video assist, and AI review. Approved drafts appear as cards.

Step 09 - Content requests

Approval heads review submissions on Requests: approve, reject, or request improvements. Demo: CRQ-00001 (12 pager).

Step 10 - Calendar

Schedule publishes, campaign dates, and deliverable due dates on Calendar. Use Schedule or day-level Add for new slots.

Step 11 - Brand kit

Configure logo variants, colors, fonts, and voice on Brand kit so Content Studio and captions stay on-brand.

Step 12 - Analytics

Review workspace usage on Analytics: campaigns, active count, content requests, drafts, approved, calendar upcoming, and research reports.

Step 13 - Microsoft 365

Open Microsoft 365 for campaign Teams workspaces, SharePoint libraries, and OneDrive draft registration. Graph provisions when Entra credentials are configured.

Step 14 - Campaign deliverables

From Campaigns, open a row to manage deliverables. Demo: etciso has brochure 12 pager (creator Deeksha Sharma, approval Prarthana Gupta).

Step 15 - Campaign home

Open the campaign name for the campaign home: brief, KPIs, captions, calendar, tasks, SharePoint assets, and open approvals. Demo home for etciso.

Roles and responsibilities

Module admin / Marketing head

  • Create campaigns and assign Users
  • Own Brand kit and Social connections
  • Monitor Operations and Workload
  • Configure Microsoft 365 collaboration

Content creator

  • Complete My Work and Tasks deliverables
  • Generate drafts in Content Studio
  • Attach brochure / video submission links
  • Respond to revision requests

Approval head

  • Approve or reject on Requests
  • Request improvements with comments
  • Schedule approved captions on Calendar
  • Review Analytics after publish
